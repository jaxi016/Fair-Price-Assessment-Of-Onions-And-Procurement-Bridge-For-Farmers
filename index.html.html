How the demo decides

A — visibly larger, rounder, healthy-colour bulbs
B — average marketable bulbs
C — visibly small or irregular bulbs

Weighted value = grade mix × configured ₹/kg × mass. The time factor decreases by the configured rate and never goes below the retained-value floor.
